# Threadwise — Product Strategy (Summary)

> Full detail is in `01_product_strategy.md`. This file is the one-page reference.

## Vision
**Threadwise makes professionals feel like they have a perfect memory.**

## Problem
Professionals managing 10–50 ongoing relationships have no single place to hold the full context of each relationship — what was said, what was promised, and what needs to happen next. Context scatters across email, chat, notes, and memory. The result: dropped follow-ups, cold relationships, and the dreaded "remind me where we were."

## Solution
A lightweight relationship-memory app built around the **thread** primitive — a living record of a professional relationship. The Attention Engine surfaces what needs attention today. AI generates context summaries before meetings. Going-Cold detection alerts before a relationship cools.

## Target Market
Independent relationship managers: consultants, founders, freelancers, account managers. Managing 10–50 active threads. Too much for a notebook; don't need a full CRM.

**North Star:** Weekly Active Threads per user.

## Business Model
| Tier | Price | Key Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 threads, 25 people |
| Pro | $12/month | Unlimited, AI, extension |
| Team | $30/user/month | Shared threads (Phase 3) |

## Roadmap
- **Phase 0 (Hackathon):** Today view, Threads, Quick Capture, Commitments, Pre-Meeting Brief, Going-Cold
- **Phase 1 (Beta, M1–3):** Auth, offline, push notifications, Live Capture, Search
- **Phase 2 (Launch, M4–6):** Browser Extension, Email digest, Calendar integration, 2,000 Pro users
- **Phase 3 (Scale, M7–18):** Team tier, shared threads, native mobile, 15,000 paid users

## Guiding Principles
1. Relationship-first, task-second
2. Surface, don't require
3. Friction is the enemy
4. Opinionated defaults
5. Privacy by design
6. No feature without a job
