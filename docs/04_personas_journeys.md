## Hero Persona: Meera

### Profile

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Meera Krishnamurthy |
| Age | 34 |
| Role | Senior Product Consultant |
| Company | Independent (client roster of 4 active clients) |
| Location | Bangalore, India |
| Devices | MacBook Pro (primary), iPhone (secondary), iPad (occasional) |

### Context
Meera runs a boutique product consultancy. She works with 4 active clients simultaneously, each of whom has a primary stakeholder she manages, plus a network of 30+ contacts she needs to stay warm with for future pipeline. She is excellent at her work but struggles with the operational overhead of relationship management.

### Current Toolstack
- **Notion**: Meeting notes (but not consistently linked to contacts)
- **Gmail labels**: Rough thread organisation (breaks down for long threads)
- **Calendar (Google)**: Scheduling only, no context attached
- **Phone contacts**: Chaotic, no notes, no thread history
- **Physical notebook**: For real-time meeting capture (doesn't sync anywhere)

### Goals
1. Never drop the ball on a client commitment
2. Always walk into a meeting feeling prepared
3. Maintain warm relationships with her network without it feeling like work
4. Have a clear view of what needs her attention each morning

### Frustrations
1. "I spend 15 minutes before every client call trying to remember where we left off."
2. "I've missed follow-ups because the action item was buried in a Notion page I forgot existed."
3. "I know I should check in with [name] but I keep forgetting until it's awkward."
4. "My notes are scattered — some in Notion, some in my notebook, some in email drafts."
5. "I tried a CRM once. It felt like it was designed for a sales team of 20, not one consultant."

### Jobs to Be Done
1. **When** I'm about to hop on a client call, **I want** a 30-second context refresh **so that** I don't start with "So, remind me where we were."
2. **When** I make a promise in a meeting, **I want** to capture it immediately **so that** I don't forget and embarrass myself.
3. **When** I open my laptop in the morning, **I want** to know exactly what needs my attention today **so that** I can prioritise without spending 20 minutes reviewing everything.
4. **When** a relationship is going cold, **I want** to be alerted **so that** I can re-engage before it becomes awkward.
5. **When** I have a bunch of thoughts after a meeting, **I want** to dump them quickly **so that** I can stop holding them in my head.

### Behaviour Patterns
- Checks her phone while commuting (7–9 AM) — high value window for digest
- Does deep work 10 AM–1 PM — do not interrupt with notifications
- Preps for afternoon calls between 2–3 PM — prime moment for Pre-Meeting Brief
- End-of-day review 5–6 PM — good moment for commitment triage

### Quotes
> "I'm not trying to build a database of my relationships. I just want to stop feeling like I'm always catching up."

> "The moment I have to spend more than 60 seconds setting something up, I'll do it in a notebook instead."

## Secondary Journey: Morning Ritual

**Scenario:** Meera opens Threadwise at 8:15 AM on a Tuesday.

1. **Notification arrived at 8:00 AM**: "You have 3 threads needing attention today."
2. **Opens Today view**: Sees Fintech Co. (2 open commitments), Startup Y (going cold), Investor Z (meeting in 3 hours).
3. **Reviews Fintech Co.**: Taps thread → Commitments tab → marks "Send revised proposal" as done.
4. **Handles Startup Y**: Taps "Re-engage" → reads the one-line context → fires off a message on WhatsApp → comes back → taps "Captured" → Quick Capture: "Sent check-in message, expect reply by EOD."
5. **Reviews Investor Z**: Taps Pre-Meeting Brief → scans in 2 minutes → adds a personal note: "Ask about portfolio company X."
6. **Brain Dump**: Has a few loose thoughts from yesterday → opens Brain Dump → types 6 entries → AI assigns 5 to threads → 1 goes to Inbox.
7. **Done in 15 minutes.** Feels in control.

## Secondary Journey: In-Meeting Capture

**Scenario:** Meera is on a 45-minute video call with Aditya (Fintech Co.).

1. Opens Threadwise on her phone (secondary device during call).
2. Taps "Start Live Session" on the Fintech Co. thread.
3. Types notes as Aditya speaks:
   - "Aditya: budget approved for Q3" → auto-tags as Note
   - "Send updated timeline by Friday" → taps Tab → becomes Commitment
   - "/decision: Go with Option B for the payment flow" → tagged as Decision
4. Call ends. "End Session" tapped → reviews 6 captures.
5. Confirms all → all saved to Fintech Co. timeline.
6. 30 seconds later: "Where We Left Off" is auto-updated.
