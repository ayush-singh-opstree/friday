## The Story

### Logline
*"Threadwise is the relationship memory layer for professionals — it holds the context of every important conversation so you never drop the ball and never have to say 'Sorry, remind me where we were.'"*

### Opening Hook (30 seconds)
> "Think about the last time you said 'remind me where we were' to a colleague or client. Or the last time you realised you'd forgotten a follow-up until it was too late. Or the last time you spent 15 minutes digging through emails to prepare for a call. That's not a memory problem. That's a tooling problem. And that's what Threadwise solves."

### Problem Statement (1 minute)
Professionals who manage work through relationships — consultants, founders, account managers, freelancers — live in the gap between their tools. Meetings happen in one place. Notes end up somewhere else. Action items scatter across emails, chats, and sticky notes. By the time they need to follow up, the context is gone.

The result: dropped balls, awkward re-introductions, relationships that go cold. Not because people don't care, but because their tools don't help them remember.

### Solution Statement (1 minute)
Threadwise introduces a simple primitive: the **thread** — a living record of a professional relationship. Every conversation, every note, every promise lives in a single place. An attention engine tells you what needs your focus today. AI surfaces the context you need before a meeting. And when a relationship starts going cold, Threadwise tells you before it's too late.

## Q&A Bank

### Product Questions

**Q: Why not just use Notion or a CRM?**
> "Notion is document-first. It's a great place to write, but it doesn't know that Aditya and Rahul are people you have ongoing relationships with, or that a sentence in your meeting notes is actually a commitment. CRMs are pipeline-first — they're designed for sales teams tracking deal stages, not individual professionals tracking relationship texture. Threadwise is the layer between those two worlds."

**Q: How is this different from a contact manager like Google Contacts?**
> "Google Contacts stores who someone is. Threadwise holds everything that's happened between you. It's the difference between a phonebook and a relationship journal."

**Q: What if I already have years of notes in Notion?**
> "You can import them over time, or just start fresh with new threads. The value compounds quickly — even 2 weeks of captures gives the AI enough context to generate a useful summary."

**Q: Why not just use AI to summarise my email?**
> "Email summarisers exist, but they only see email. Your most important professional context is scattered across email, Slack, phone calls, in-person meetings, and your own notes. Threadwise is the place where all of that comes together by design."

### Business Questions

**Q: What's the business model?**
> "Freemium. Free tier: up to 5 active threads, good for trying the product. Pro at $12/month: unlimited threads, AI features, browser extension. Team tier at $30/user/month in Phase 3 for shared threads and team workspaces."

**Q: How do you acquire users?**
> "Content-led. We write for the jobs: 'How to prepare for a client meeting in 30 seconds', 'How to stop dropping follow-ups'. The audience is searchable and willing to try tools. Referral from power users in the target segment."

**Q: How do you handle churn?**
> "The value compounds over time — the more captures in a thread, the better the AI summaries, the more the user depends on the context. We also design for habit formation: daily digest, commitment reminders, going-cold alerts. All of these pull users back on a daily cadence."

### Technical Questions

**Q: Is my relationship data safe?**
> "Yes. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We don't sell your data. We don't share contact data with third parties. Users can export all their data as JSON at any time and request permanent deletion."

**Q: What AI model powers the summaries?**
> "GPT-4o for summaries and briefs. The context sent to the AI is only the user's own captures — no contact data, no full names unless the user's own notes contain them. We're exploring on-device models for a fully private mode in the future."

**Q: Does it work offline?**
> "Yes. Captures are saved locally first (IndexedDB) and synced when you reconnect. You can use the app fully offline and your data will sync automatically."

## Recovery Plans

### Recovery Plan: AI is Down

**Symptom:** Pre-Meeting Brief or Where We Left Off fails to generate.

**Response:**
1. Switch to the fallback pre-prepared screenshot on the device.
2. Say: "The AI integration is live but occasionally needs a moment — what you'd see here is a structured brief drawn from the thread's captures. Let me show you the manual fallback."
3. Show: Last 5 captures from the thread, open commitments list — explain this is the degraded-mode view.

---

### Recovery Plan: App Won't Load

**Symptom:** The app is blank, errored, or stuck loading.

**Response:**
1. Hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R).
2. If still broken: switch to the backup device with the app pre-loaded.
3. If backup unavailable: switch to Figma prototype (have it open and ready).
4. Say: "While I get this sorted, let me walk you through the experience in the prototype — the logic is the same."

---

### Recovery Plan: Seed Data Missing

**Symptom:** The app loads but shows empty states or the wrong data.

**Response:**
1. If demo account is wrong: log out, log back in as Meera (credentials pre-saved).
2. If seed data was accidentally deleted: have a reset script ready (`npm run seed`) — run it if you have a terminal handy.
3. If neither is available: tell the story using screenshots on phone while narrating.

---

### Recovery Plan: Nervousness / Going Blank

**Response:**
1. Return to the logline: "Threadwise holds the context of every important professional relationship."
2. Open the Today view. Point at it. Say: "This tells you what matters today, without you having to figure it out."
3. Open a thread. Point at the Brief tab. Say: "This gives you 30 seconds of prep that used to take 15 minutes."
4. That's the whole pitch. Everything else is detail.
